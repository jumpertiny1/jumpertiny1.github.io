01 / 04

Amazon Delivery Performance Analysis

SQL (MySQL) · Power BI · DAX

Confound-controlled analysis of 43,700 Amazon deliveries — separating what actually slows an order down from what merely correlates with it.

Fig. 1 — Power BI dashboard, built on a cleaned MySQL database of 43,700 orders.

Grocery orders deliver roughly 5x faster than every other category — confirmed at the individual order level, not an averaging artifact. Traffic, not distance, turned out to be the dominant driver of delay.
